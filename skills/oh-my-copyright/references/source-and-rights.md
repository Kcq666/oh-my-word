# Source and rights

The source manifest is an inventory, not a license grant. Select files for their real business responsibility and keep exact paths, SHA-256 hashes, line ranges and reasons. Do not select dependency, generated, build, credential, private-key, binary or symlink content. Source hashes, safe relative paths, line counts and credential scans are rechecked during lint and extraction.

Rights holder, ownership basis and applicant fields must be confirmed by the user. Source code cannot prove ownership, employment, assignment or eligibility. A clean manifest is not legal advice.
